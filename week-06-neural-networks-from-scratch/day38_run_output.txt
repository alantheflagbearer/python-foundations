======================================================================
1. THE OVERFITTING PROBLEM
======================================================================
Train set: 20 points, Val set: 10 points
Network: 64 hidden units -- deliberately oversized for 20 training points.

Epoch 0:    train_loss=0.5921  val_loss=0.6889
Epoch 500:  train_loss=0.2657  val_loss=0.2692
Epoch 204: val_loss hits its BEST value = 0.2690
Epoch 2999: train_loss=0.1737  val_loss=0.4515
Final train accuracy: 0.900
Final val accuracy:   0.900
Train loss keeps falling toward 0 while val loss bottoms out early and
climbs back up -- the network is memorizing the 20 training points instead
of learning the underlying pattern.

======================================================================
2. L2 REGULARIZATION (WEIGHT DECAY)
======================================================================
lam=0.02 -- Epoch 2999: train_loss=0.3567  val_loss=0.3749
Best val_loss=0.3587 at epoch 45
Final train accuracy: 0.850
Final val accuracy:   0.800
Penalizing large weights keeps the decision boundary simpler, so val loss
no longer climbs back up the way it did with no regularization.

======================================================================
3. DROPOUT
======================================================================
keep_prob=0.6 -- Epoch 2999: train_loss=0.2374  val_loss=0.3697
Best val_loss=0.2624 at epoch 222
Final train accuracy: 0.900
Final val accuracy:   0.900
Randomly zeroing 40% of hidden units each step forces the network to not
rely on any single unit -- a different way of fighting the same problem.

======================================================================
4. EARLY STOPPING
======================================================================
Training stopped at epoch 405 (patience=200 epochs with no val_loss improvement)
Best val_loss=0.2690 at epoch 204
train_loss at stop=0.2668  val_loss at stop=0.2691
Final train accuracy: 0.900
Final val accuracy:   0.900
No change to the loss function or the network at all -- just watching val
loss and refusing to keep training once it stops paying off.

WHAT THIS MAPS TO IN PYTORCH
======================================================================
nn.Dropout(p=0.4)                          # keep_prob=0.6 here
optim.SGD(params, lr=0.5, weight_decay=0.02) # L2, same as lam=0.02 here
# early stopping has no built-in class -- it's the same manual
# best-val-loss-and-patience loop used in demo_early_stopping() above

PyTorch's nn.Dropout automatically does nothing at eval time (net.eval()),
the same on/off switch this script implements by hand with the
`training=True/False` flag on forward().
